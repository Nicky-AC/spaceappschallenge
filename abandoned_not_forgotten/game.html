A game from Sentinels of Silence

Sentinel Run

You are an astronaut on a long walk past the silent machines. Jump over the rocks and the craters. Every time you fall, one of the machines tells you something it discovered.

Distance 0 m · Best 0 m
How to playPress the space bar or tap the game to jump. Hold a little longer to jump higher.
Moon or Mars?Gravity is weaker on the Moon than on Mars, so on the Moon you jump higher and come down more slowly.
Look behind youThe shapes on the horizon are the real machines from the map, each one in its own world.

The facts shown in the game are the same ones as on the story cards of the map, taken from the NASA pages listed there. The landscape and the distances in the game are a drawing, not a map.

Made by team Centinela del Silencio for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. Code and first drafts of the texts written with AI assistance (Claude, by Anthropic) and checked by the author. Not endorsed by NASA.
